About Us

Who we are and what we stand for

Our Story

Lifeline Medical Centre opened its doors in 2008 as a small clinic with three doctors and a single consulting room. Today we run eight departments, a fully equipped laboratory and a 24-hour emergency unit, serving thousands of patients across Anambra State and beyond.

What has not changed is our belief that good healthcare should be accessible, affordable and delivered with respect for every patient who walks through our doors.

Our Mission

To provide quality, patient-centred healthcare services that are accessible to the communities we serve, delivered by qualified professionals using modern equipment and evidence-based practice.

Our Values

  • Compassion — we treat every patient with dignity and care
  • Competence — our staff are qualified, licensed and continuously trained
  • Integrity — clear pricing, honest diagnosis, no hidden charges
  • Accessibility — extended hours and Saturday clinics for working patients
  • Confidentiality — your medical records stay private
